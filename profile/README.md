### The problem we solve

Small service firms lose money between the first enquiry and the paid consultation. Leads arrive from forms, ads, WhatsApp and email; replies take hours; follow-ups depend on memory; and nobody can see the pipeline.

We fix that with small, reliable systems, built and proven on our own group of UK consultancies first.

### What we build

| | Service | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| ⚡ | **Speed-to-lead** | Every enquiry answered on WhatsApp or email within a minute, qualified and offered a booking |
| 🗂️ | **CRM & pipeline** | One source of truth, lead scoring, routing to the right person, automatic chasing |
| 💳 | **Paid consultation flow** | Booking, payment, reminders and no-show recovery |
| 📋 | **Compliance tracking** | Deadline alerts, checklists and audit trails for regulated work |
| 🤖 | **AI assistants** | Triage, document review, drafting and reporting, with a person approving anything client-facing |
| 📈 | **Reporting** | A weekly pipeline report per brand: what came in, what converted, where it leaked |

### How we work

1. **Fix the biggest leak first.** Measure, then automate one step.
2. **Many forms, one database.**
3. **Humans approve, machines prepare.**
4. **GDPR by design.** Minimum data, no client data in logs.
5. **Everything fails loudly.** Broken workflows alert a person the same day.
